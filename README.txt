Bring supported TP-Link Kasa plugs, power strips, wall switches, dimmers, bulbs, and light strips into Homey Pro for responsive local control. Use device controls and Flows for switching, dimming, lighting, and energy insights while keeping everyday communication on your local network.

Pair devices by automatic discovery or IP address, with optional recovery after an address change. Newer KS225, S500D, and KS240 devices can use TP-Link account credentials to authenticate their encrypted local connection. Available controls and measurements vary by model.
